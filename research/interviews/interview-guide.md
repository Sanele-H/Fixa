# Interview guide (Role 4 leads, everyone helps)

**Goal:** talk to 10 tradespeople and 10 households this week (most of them on Tue 29 Sep). Good places to find people: residence maintenance staff, hardware stores, neighbours and family.

## Before you start

- Say who you are and that it's for a student hackathon project.
- Ask: "May we quote your words in our pitch, without your name?" Write down the answer.
- Don't write down names, phone numbers or addresses. Give each person a code instead: `T01` for a tradesperson, `H01` for a household.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you hired someone for a repair, or the last job you found. How did it happen?
2. Has language ever caused a misunderstanding about the problem, the price or the time?
3. Have you ever felt unsafe, or been scammed, when hiring someone or going to a job?
4. *(Tradespeople)* How did you get your first customers? What proof of skill do you have?
5. Would you use an app for this? What would stop you?
6. What would you pay for a safe booking, and when?

## After each interview

Add one row to `interview-log.csv` within the hour, while you still remember exact words. Store raw notes in `research/interviews/raw/`. That folder is git-ignored, so the notes stay off GitHub.
