# Questions judges will probably ask

One person answers each topic. Practise on Fri 2 Oct.

| Question | Short answer | Proof to point at | Answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| How do you make money? | Customer booking fee at unlock, bulk buyers, and placement partners. Providers never pay. | `business-model.md` | Role 4 |
| Won't everyone just move to WhatsApp after the first job? | Some will, and that's fine. Logging off-app jobs keeps growing the provider's record, which wins them new customers and later supports an ARPL application. | — | Role 4 |
| Isn't showing newcomers unfair to customers? | Newcomers start on small, low-risk jobs, and we measured the effect on quality. | Fairness chart + average rating | Role 4 |
| How is this different from Kandua or Snupit? | Local languages, details that unlock only after both approve, and a real way in for newcomers. | — | Role 4 |
| How good is the translation really? | How many prices, times and trade terms each backend broke, and how protection fixed them. | 30-message results | Role 1 |
| What about POPIA? | We store verification results, not ID photos or biometrics. Details are shared only after both agree, and the server enforces it. | `backend/fixa/domain/job_states.py` | Role 3 |
| Is there real login? | Not in the demo. The demo uses fixed demo users. Real auth is on the roadmap. | — | Role 3 |
